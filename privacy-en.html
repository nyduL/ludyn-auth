Ludyn Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026.

1. Scope

This policy explains how Ludyn handles data needed for authentication, library synchronization, gamer profile, recommendations, Wishlist, Free/Pro features, purchases and rewarded advertising.

2. Data we process

  • Account: internal identifier, email and display name.
  • Library and behavior: games, linked platforms, playtime when available, ratings, Lifecycle, achievements and events used for synchronization and Personal Intelligence.
  • Integrations: platform/account identifiers strictly required for enabled integrations.
  • Purchases: subscription status and technical identifiers. Ludyn does not persist raw Google Play purchase tokens.
  • Rewarded Ads: opaque identifiers and data required for server-side reward validation and fraud/replay prevention.
  • Operations and security: technical logs, request IDs and minimum information required to diagnose failures and abuse.

3. Purposes

We use this data to operate accounts, synchronize data, import authorized libraries, calculate compatibility and recommendations, enforce quotas, validate purchases, unlock Pro features, validate rewards and protect the service.

4. Service providers

Ludyn may use Supabase, Render, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and game-platform APIs when the user chooses to link them. Each provider operates under its own terms and policies.

5. Sharing

Ludyn does not sell personal data. Data is shared only as required to operate the providers above, comply with law or protect service security.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app or through the web deletion resource. Account data is deleted except where limited retention is required for law, security, fraud prevention or financial records.

Delete your Ludyn account and data

7. Security

We use authentication, per-user isolation, Row Level Security, least privilege, server-side purchase/reward validation, HTTPS and controls that reduce credential/data exposure.

8. Children

Ludyn is not specifically directed to children. Store age and audience declarations will reflect the features actually released.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as features, providers or legal requirements change.
